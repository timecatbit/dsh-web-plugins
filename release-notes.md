**DSH Pocket UI 0.1.20 and DSH Passkey Proxy 0.1.11** keep phone settings pages inside the screen, with the same margin on both sides.

- **Pocket UI:** On a phone, settings content started 32 px from the left edge (48 px on `0.2.1-rc.5`) but ran past the right edge. On **General**, the dropdowns touched the screen edge and the third **Appearance** button was cut off. The plugin padded each settings section on top of DSH's own padding, and DSH sizes those sections to the full width, so the extra padding pushed their right edge off the screen. Every settings page now keeps 20 px on both sides. On **Built-in plugins**, the agent preset switcher moves to its own line when it does not fit beside **Session plugins**.
- **Passkey Proxy:** Status values on **Settings → LAN Proxy** never wrapped, so long ones such as "Setup required (unauthenticated requests blocked)" ran past the card in narrow windows. They now wrap inside their column, each address stays on one line with its status light, and labels keep room for a word or two. Status badges such as **Enabled** now line up with the other values instead of sitting 8 px to their left.

Measured on every settings page, in English and Chinese, in WebKit and Chromium at iPhone SE, iPhone 15 and iPhone 15 Pro Max sizes, on standalone DSH Web `0.2.1-rc.5`, `0.2.2-rc.1`, `0.2.2-rc.2` and `0.2.2-rc.3`. Password sign-in through the proxy, the WebSocket connection, the settings title, composer focus and the wide desktop layout were rechecked. One exception: on `0.2.1-rc.5`, whose settings window is narrower, the longest address on **LAN Proxy** reaches 5 px into the card's inner padding at iPhone SE width.

Update both plugins through [DSH Discover](https://github.com/timecatbit/dsh-web-plugins#安装与更新).
